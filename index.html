Welcome to Sinhgad Public School Korti

Sinhgad Public School - a Senior Secondary School was established in 2010 at Korti, Pandharpur to provide quality education to the children of this rural area. The school ensures the highest quality of faculty, resources and infrastructure and helps to build a society that is vibrant and growing through sharing and enhancing knowledge.

Mission Statement

Sinhgad Public School Korti -Pandharpur strives to be a community of Learners in which all stake holders are collectively engaged with challenging academic, physical, emotional, social and safe environment where everyone can learn and respect each other. We dare to use innovative techniques to enhance lifelong learning through technology, intelligence tools and instructional strategies. We will provide each student with the application based knowledge, skill and positive attitude to become a productive and responsible citizen in a challenging, technological and diverse world. We plan to adopt all cultural background and diversities to nurture growth, responsibility and productivity by promoting sportsmanship, school spirit and pride in our self. We are also committed to promote and enhance the educational growth in our neighbouring rural areas.

Affiliation Status

The School got sanction for Extension of Affiliation of the Central Board of Secondary Education (C.B.S.E.), New Delhi for a period of Five years w. e. f 01/04/2026 to 31/03/2031.

  • The School's Affiliation number is 1130295 and School number is 30210.
  • The School got upgraded to Senior Secondary Level in 2013.

School Curriculum

The school is affiliated with C.B.S.E. and follows the courses and rules lay down by it. The school has adopted books based on C.B.S.E. and N.C.E.R.T. (Govt. of India)

Welcome to Sinhgad Public School Korti

Sinhgad Public School Korti is committed to providing quality education in a supportive and inspiring environment. We encourage students to learn, explore, innovate and develop their unique talents.

Our focus is not only on academic excellence but also on character development, discipline, creativity, communication and overall personality development.

Pre – Primary Section

Every child seeking admission should visit the school in person. The admission of the child would be determined on the basis of the observations during his school visit.

Primary and Secondary Section

Students would be admitted on the basis of the performance in the written test and personal interview. The decision of the Principal is final regarding the admission.

Admission will be confirmed after completion of the necessary office procedure and payment of the fees.

Documents to be Submitted

Pre-primary Section

The original Birth Certificate.

Std. I to IX

The School leaving certificate from the last school attended.

Progress report of the last qualifying examination.
